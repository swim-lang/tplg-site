The Firm

Political law for teams trying to move things forward.

Political work moves fast. The rules do not always make the path obvious. TPLG helps clients understand what is possible, what is risky, and what needs to happen next — whether they are launching a campaign, drafting a ballot measure, responding to an investigation, or keeping a complex operation compliant.

Practice Areas — Six Areas of Counsel

Support across the full political lifecycle.

From front-end compliance to enforcement defense, TPLG supports the legal work behind campaigns, committees, ballot measures, advocacy efforts, and political organizations.

I.Campaign Finance

Compliance, contribution limits, coordination rules, reporting, and committee guidance.

II.Compliance + Reporting

Ongoing filings, deadlines, treasury support, audits, and internal systems.

III.Enforcement Defense

Investigations, agency matters, audits, enforcement actions, and corrective strategy.

IV.Ballot Measures

Drafting, qualification, legal strategy, disclosure, and campaign counsel.

V.Lobbying + Ethics

Lobbying registration, disclosure, gift rules, conflicts, and public ethics guidance.

VI.Election Law

Ballot access, candidate issues, election disputes, and political litigation.
